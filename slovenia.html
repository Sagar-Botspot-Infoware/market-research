The dated trigger

B2B e-invoicing becomes mandatory on 1 January 2028

The ZIERDED act (adopted 23 Oct 2025) applies to every business in the Slovenian Business Register and to sole traders. From that date, paper and PDF invoices are no longer allowed between companies, and invoices may not be sent by email — they go through e-invoice providers, Peppol, direct systems, or the free miniBlagajna app. SEEN gov.si

In 2022, about 60% of Slovenian firms with 10+ employees sent e-invoices (57% small, 74% medium, 86% large) — so roughly 40% did not. SEEN gov.si

What the numbers say
  • Odoo is a small ecosystem in Slovenia. 6 partners against PANTHEON's 21. The largest Odoo partner by revenue (Editor, €1.00M) is about 1/20th the size of Seyfor (€20.87M).
  • Two local groups control accounting. Seyfor reports €20.87M + €8.08M across its two Slovenian companies; Datalab reports €14.61M. The largest ERP partners are the Microsoft Dynamics and SAP houses (€13–20M each).
  • Global SaaS runs the website-facing modules, not local vendors. WooCommerce ~50% of stores, Mailchimp 78% of email-tool sites, WordPress 75% of CMS sites. Odoo's web footprint: 71 stores and 50 websites.
  • Odoo's localization gap is narrower than assumed, but real. Core covers Slovenian accounting and taxes. Payroll (REK-O), fiscal cash registers and eSLOG depend on third-party modules.
  • Odoo's existing Slovenian base clusters in two industries: Manufacturing/Maintenance (36) and Wholesale/Retail (35), by the partners' published reference tags.
