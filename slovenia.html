The dated trigger

B2B e-invoicing becomes mandatory on 1 January 2028

The ZIERDED act (adopted 23 Oct 2025) applies to every business in the Slovenian Business Register and to sole traders. From that date, paper and PDF invoices are no longer allowed between companies, and invoices may not be sent by email. They must go through e-invoice providers, own direct systems, Peppol, or the free miniBlagajna app. SEEN gov.si

In 2022, about 60% of Slovenian firms with 10+ employees sent e-invoices (57% small, 74% medium, 86% large), so roughly 40% did not. SEEN gov.si

What the numbers say
  • Odoo is a small ecosystem in Slovenia. It has 6 partners against PANTHEON's 21. The largest Odoo partner by revenue (Editor, €1.00M) is about 1/20th the size of Seyfor (€20.87M).
  • Two local groups control accounting. Seyfor reports €20.87M + €8.08M across its two Slovenian companies (not consolidated). Datalab reports €14.61M. The largest ERP partners are the Microsoft Dynamics and SAP houses (€13–20M each).
  • Global SaaS runs the website-facing modules, not local vendors. WooCommerce has 50% of stores, Mailchimp 78% of email-tool sites, WordPress 75% of CMS sites. No local vendor appeared in email, marketing or chat. Odoo's web footprint: 71 stores and 50 websites.
  • Odoo's localization gap is narrower than assumed, but real. Core covers Slovenian accounting and taxes. Payroll (REK-O), fiscal cash registers and eSLOG depend on third-party modules, while SAOP and Minimax document REK-O payroll themselves.
  • Odoo's existing Slovenian base clusters in two industries: Manufacturing/Maintenance (36) and Wholesale/Retail (35), by the partners' published reference tags.
Method & limits

SEEN = the source page was opened and read (key claims re-checked on 25 Nov 2026). SEARCH = the figure appears in a search-result extract; the page itself was not opened. UNKNOWN = no source found. Nothing on this page is estimated.

Website-scan limits: aguko and W3Techs scan only .si domains, so Slovenian firms on .com/.eu are missed. Website detection also cannot see back-office tools (accounting, payroll, manufacturing). wmtips and BuiltWith blocked automated reads.

Revenue: total revenue, not net sales. Group figures are not added across companies unless stated.

What would turn UNKNOWNs into numbers: the UJP e-invoice provider list (PDF), the OpenPeppol access-point directory, the paid 6Wresearch Slovenia ERP report, and the Microsoft and SAP partner directories read directly.
