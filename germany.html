The dated triggers

Two legal clocks already running

B2B e-invoicing. Every German business has had to receive structured e-invoices since 1 Jan 2025. From 1 Jan 2027 firms with >€800k turnover must issue them (XRechnung / ZUGFeRD / Peppol); from 1 Jan 2028 nearly all. Confirmed vs EU Commission factsheet + statute §27 UStG. SEEN

POS fiscal (KassenSichV). Every electronic till needs a certified TSE (§146a AO) since 2020; electronic reporting to the Finanzamt since 1 Jan 2025. SEEN

Receive → 2025Issue >€800k → 2027Issue all → 2028Odoo Community already produces XRechnung
What the numbers say
  • Odoo is near-invisible at the website layer, and that's the wrong place to look. ~1,648 .de sites, zero detections in CRM, email or live chat — but these scanners only see public websites. Installed back-office ERP is structurally invisible to them, so this is not evidence of low ERP adoption.
  • German accounting runs on DATEV, and DATEV is not really a rival — it's the rail. Every SME's Steuerberater already works in DATEV. Odoo in Germany lives or dies by its DATEV export — which Odoo gates behind Enterprise.
  • Odoo already fits the e-invoicing law for free. Community ships SKR03/SKR04, a GoBD audit trail, and Factur-X/XRechnung — exactly what the 2027/2028 mandate needs. The cleanest wedge.
  • German payroll is a hard gap. Absent from Community and Odoo's own Enterprise localizations; only a paid third-party app. Both an opening and a risk.
  • The back-office categories have no measured data. WMS and Manufacturing/MES adoption are UNKNOWN in Germany — only conflicting vendor claims. Don't quote a share there; nobody can.
  • Where Odoo's German base sits: Manufacturing, Wholesale/Retail and IT — the three verticals its 108 partners cluster in.
Method & limits

SEEN = source page opened and read (25 Nov 2026). SEARCH = figure from a search-result snippet; page not opened. UNKNOWN = no source found. Nothing here is estimated.

Website-scan limits: aguko and W3Techs scan only .de domains and see only public-page tech — blind to ERP, payroll and WMS. aguko "%" = share among sites where that category was detected, not share of all .de sites.

Revenue: North Data blocked every automated read and paywalls private-firm revenue, so mid/small vendors are UNKNOWN. Big-vendor figures are self-reported across different fiscal years; SAP Germany is not broken out.
